# shell as root by modify /etc/sudoers

登录`loneferret`用户后，`sudo -l`发现是一个白名单策略，仅允许执行`/usr/local/bin/ht`

![](assets/images/2025-09-15-Kiptrox-1.3-Walkthrough/68f5f08d-ea66-4b01-b325-7bf4e82b0ba7.png)

执行`sudo /usr/local/bin/ht`报错`Error opening terminal: xterm-256color.` 代表当前的`TERM`环境变量传递的终端配置是不被`ht`命令所允许的，在`expoert TERM=xterm`后重新执行，弹出窗口

![](assets/images/2025-09-15-Kiptrox-1.3-Walkthrough/4e840347-7dd4-456f-bff6-0ce863cf3c4a.png)

这个窗口允许编辑文件，通过键入`F3`输入`/etc/sudoers`编辑`sudo`权限，将`loneferret`用户sudo权限改为`ALL=(ALL) ALL`

![](assets/images/2025-09-15-Kiptrox-1.3-Walkthrough/e625d937-05fa-4f13-bb4d-0b9b7c9b0ab0.png)

`F2`保存,`F10`退出，然后`sudo su`完成提权

![](assets/images/2025-09-15-Kiptrox-1.3-Walkthrough/7ca537ca-c8f8-4e7e-9c80-83aaa0bc11d4.png)
