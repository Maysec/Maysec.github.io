# shell as root by password reuse

例行检查，发现`root`以外的两个有效用户`mysql`和`dan`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-18-pWnOS2.0-Walkthrough/da8b6359-a2fb-42df-8d48-7f59898a3c5a.png)

在`/var/www`目录下发现`mysqli_connect.php`，使用其中的密码却无法登录

通过`find`查找发现`/var/`目录下存在另一个`mysqli_connect.php`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-18-pWnOS2.0-Walkthrough/28658254-ecba-45b0-96fb-ba02a8466be5.png)

使用其中泄露的凭证成功登录`mysql`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-18-pWnOS2.0-Walkthrough/ad145d5d-b73a-4eac-8574-3f3cc2ace9b3.png)

![](../assets/images/2025-09-18-pWnOS2.0-Walkthrough/0c988fe4-e0ee-4f33-b2f1-d615e38ede57.png)

其`users`表中包含了用户`dan`和`admin`的密码`hash`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-18-pWnOS2.0-Walkthrough/4f24c0ab-6187-44ab-a137-c37024f165a9.png)

通过`hashes.com`爆破得到用户`dan`密码`killerbeesareflying`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-18-pWnOS2.0-Walkthrough/bee6b815-0957-4127-8abd-ddf26974751c.png)

经过一番尝试，发现数据库中的密码似乎没什么用，最后通过密码碰撞发现`/var/mysqli_connect.php`中的数据库密码就是root密码

![](../assets/images/2025-09-18-pWnOS2.0-Walkthrough/51526851-87e8-4c00-95bd-e16b45ec741d.png)
