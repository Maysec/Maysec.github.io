# shell as Ori by Portknocking → ftp → hydra

通过`wireshark`抓包，能发现靶机从`tcp/1337`端口在向`kali`发送`Port Knocking`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/839497f7-e7dc-418e-8afa-443cc6a243ab.png)

`Port Knocking`的目标端口可能隐藏着某种信息

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/6a40635b-7bd2-4aeb-8fda-af2abdd20a69.png)

通过[`https://www.rapidtables.com/convert/number/ascii-hex-bin-dec-converter.html`](https://www.rapidtables.com/convert/number/ascii-hex-bin-dec-converter.html)解密得到`Mellon69`，可能是个密码

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/2574db8a-9e00-4f40-a7a4-51f897f6c95a.png)

爆破得到ftp凭据`Balrog:Mellon69`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/292ece26-175a-4b2f-962d-13aac25ff049.png)

ftp登陆后发现是空目录，`pwd`发现位于`/prison`目录，具有切换目录权限

在`/var/www/html`发现存在`QlVraKW4fbIkXau9zkAPNGzviT3UKntl`目录

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/65ebacc3-6922-4e3c-9247-d47e81af9889.png)

访问后发现提供了`Prisoner`囚犯姓名和密码

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/9db79fdc-687c-4a1a-b8a0-04affd452edb.png)

`crackstation`解密失败

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/29b20298-deb8-434c-a458-8aabc23f80e1.png)

源代码中发现存在`salt`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/2f2b46e5-d51f-4a48-a157-68e19c753eff.png)

构造`hashes.txt`，交给`john`爆破

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/5ea25a8a-2a50-46a5-92cf-97c71202a313.png)

将爆破得到的凭证写入文件，交给`hydra`快速验证，得到有效凭证`Ori:spanky`

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/ab18fb70-edc7-47b4-98c9-a28ee00fa960.png)

# shell as root by id\_rsa

进入`/home/Oia/.ssh`目录通过`ssh root@127.0.0.1 -i id_rsa`直接登录到`root`

由于`Oia`用户公钥存在于`/root/.ssh/authorized_keys`文件，所以可以私钥登录

![](../assets/images/2025-09-17-moria-1.1-Walkthrough/78d49657-e2b0-4951-857c-059fb7bbbcb3.png)
